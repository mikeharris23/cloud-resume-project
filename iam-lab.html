Project approach

I organized a sample identity environment around departmental access, administrative responsibilities, and role-oriented security groups. Group naming conventions such as SG-DEPT-, SG-ADMIN-, and SG-ROLE- make the intended access model easier to review and maintain. These screenshots show the created tenant objects and group structure; they do not, on their own, verify effective permissions or directory-role assignments.

Security principles practiced

The lab focuses on consistent identity inventories, separation of standard and administrative identities, clear security-group naming, and planning for role-based access and least privilege. Future modules can add evidence of access assignment, verification, authentication controls, and lifecycle processes as those tasks are completed.

Governance method and limitations

Review method: Manually compared fictional employees’ department and job-title attributes against Entra security-group memberships, directory roles, and visible application assignments. Documented simulated retain recommendations for AR-001 and AR-003; no actual business-owner or manager approval occurred.

Remediation method: For AR-004, deliberately created a cross-department group-membership exception in an isolated lab, then removed it and verified that the legitimate Finance memberships remained. The screenshots establish the visible before-and-after membership state, not downstream resource authorization or audit events.

Platform and scope: Microsoft Entra ID Free; manual governance simulation. This project does not claim that native Microsoft Entra Access Reviews, entitlement management, or privileged identity management campaigns were executed.

Evidence boundary: No independent verification of effective group permissions, application entitlements outside the visible Entra assignments, audit logs, or successful session revocation. No production accounts or real business incidents are represented.
